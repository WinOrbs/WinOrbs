# TEAM mode

TEAM rooms have two groups of five players. Players can switch to the other
group while the lobby is open, subject to its five-player limit. The first
member of each group is its leader and can set the shared team name and color
until the start countdown begins.

The server starts a 30-second countdown only when both groups have five
players. If either group loses a player during that countdown, it is cancelled
and the room returns to team selection. The lineup and team profiles are locked
once the countdown starts.

Brief connection drops can recover the same player session for up to two
minutes; the player's room seat is held during that recovery window.

An administrator can force-start a TEAM room before it is full. At least one
player must be present on each side; the admin panel displays each side's
occupancy and starts the match after a five-second countdown.

Eliminated players remain in the match as spectators. They can follow living
teammates or move a free camera with WASD/the arrow keys; touch controls are
available on mobile. Spectator input only moves the local camera and cannot
control the eliminated player.
